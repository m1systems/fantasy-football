ESPN Lineup Assistant

Drag this button to Chrome's bookmarks bar:

Copy ESPN Lineup State

  1. Open your ESPN fantasy team’s My Team page.
  2. Confirm the lineup for the week you want to analyze is displayed.
  3. Click Copy ESPN Lineup State.
  4. Paste directly into the weekly ChatGPT conversation and send. No fence or cleanup is needed.

Version 1.1 identifies the fantasy team and displayed scoring week, then extracts every populated starter, bench, and IR row currently displayed by ESPN.
