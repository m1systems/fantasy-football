ESPN Draft Assistant

Drag this button to Chrome's bookmarks bar:

Copy ESPN Draft State

  1. Open an ESPN desktop draft room and leave the Players tab selected.
  2. Keep the available-player list near the top.
  3. Click Copy ESPN Draft State.
  4. Paste directly into the mock-draft ChatGPT conversation and send. No fence or cleanup is needed.

Version 0.3 extracts the live clock, next two snake picks, your roster, the 20 most recent selections, and up to 15 currently rendered available players.
