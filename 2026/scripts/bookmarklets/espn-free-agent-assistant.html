ESPN Free Agent Assistant

Drag this button to Chrome's bookmarks bar:

Copy ESPN Free Agents

  1. Open ESPN Fantasy Football and select Players → Add Players.
  2. Apply the position, availability, and sort options you want ESPN to display.
  3. Click Copy ESPN Free Agents.
  4. Paste directly into the weekly ChatGPT conversation and send. No fence or cleanup is needed.

Version 1.2 identifies the fantasy league and displayed scoring week, then extracts every player row currently shown in ESPN’s free-agent table.
