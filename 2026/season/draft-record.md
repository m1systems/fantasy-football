## Evaluation Policy

The ESPN grade is recorded as platform feedback, not as a definitive evaluation of the roster or advisor. Assess the draft over the season using:

- starter availability and points;
- value gained or lost relative to alternatives;
- waiver flexibility created by roster construction;
- whether identified risks materialized;
- quality of decisions based on information available at the time;
- final record, points for, playoff qualification, and finish.

Do not retroactively label a sound decision poor solely because of injury or variance.
